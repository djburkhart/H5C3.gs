What H5C3.gs is

H5C3.gs stands for HTML5 CSS3 Grid System. It is a small framework for modern browsers: custom properties hold the count and the gap, and @supports checks for CSS grid before any tracks are drawn.

This page

How the 2×2 is marked up

The outer region uses two-column-grid. The content is a two-column-subgrid, so the article and the aside share one row. Change the word two to three or four and the tracks follow.
