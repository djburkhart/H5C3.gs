Project info

H5C3.gs is an HTML5 and CSS grid system. Version 2 lays pages out with fluid tracks instead of a calculated pixel width.

02

Page info

This page is the two-column example. Both cards share one row on a wide screen and stack on a narrow one.

Fluid tracks

Each column is at least the minimum size, and leftover space is shared. No breakpoint list is required.
