Four-column grid

Four tracks at full width, then three, two, and one. A span-2 card covers half the row while four tracks fit, and the whole row when only two remain.

Spans that follow the tracks

A span of two is half of this grid on a wide screen. When the grid collapses to two columns, that same span fills the row instead of overflowing it.

span-2

Half of a four-column row

Paired with the next card, this covers the left half while four columns fit. On a narrower grid it takes the whole row.

span-2

The other half

Both cards use the same span. Nothing is hard-coded to “column 3 through column 5”.
